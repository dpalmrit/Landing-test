Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Overview

PitchScout AI is a video analysis platform for youth and amateur soccer. We take privacy seriously — especially when it comes to players under 18. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

If you have questions, contact us at support@pitchscout.ai.

What we collect

We collect the minimum information needed to run the analysis and deliver your report:

  • Jersey number — used to identify the player in the footage
  • Position, age group, dominant foot, match type, kit colour — used to contextualise the analysis and grade the player against appropriate benchmarks
  • Video footage — uploaded directly to encrypted cloud storage for processing
  • Email address — used to deliver the completed report and to send a link to retrieve it
  • Session identifiers — anonymous IDs used to link your upload to your report

We do not collect player names, parent names, addresses, phone numbers, or any other personal identifying information.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

PitchScout AI is designed for use by coaches, club staff, and parents — not directly by minors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Player data is submitted by an authorised adult on the player's behalf.

We never ask for a player's name. All analysis is keyed to a jersey number only. Players are never identifiable from the data we store.

If you believe a minor's data has been submitted without appropriate authorisation, contact us at support@pitchscout.ai and we will delete it promptly.

How we handle your data

Here is exactly what happens to your data from upload to delivery:

  • Upload — Video is transferred directly to an encrypted AWS S3 bucket in the US East region using a time-limited secure URL. It is never routed through our web servers.
  • Analysis — Once uploaded, the footage is submitted to TwelveLabs (our AI video analysis provider) for processing. TwelveLabs processes the video on our behalf under a data processing agreement.
  • Report delivery — Your report is delivered by email via Amazon SES. The email contains a secure link unique to your session.
  • Storage — Report data is stored in MongoDB Atlas (encrypted at rest). Video footage is retained for a limited period after analysis to allow for re-processing if needed, then permanently deleted.
  • No sale of data — We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or third parties for marketing purposes.
  • No facial recognition — We do not use facial recognition or biometric identification at any point in our analysis pipeline.

Third-party services

PitchScout AI relies on the following third-party providers to deliver the service. Each is bound by its own privacy policy and, where applicable, a data processing agreement with us:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — cloud infrastructure, video storage (S3), and email delivery (SES)
  • TwelveLabs — AI video analysis and understanding
  • MongoDB Atlas — encrypted database for report and session storage
  • Amazon Cognito — authentication for club and coach accounts

Data retention

We retain data for the minimum period necessary:

  • Video footage — deleted after analysis is complete and the report has been delivered
  • Report data — retained for as long as your account or report link is active. You can request deletion at any time.
  • Email addresses — retained only to deliver your report. We do not send marketing emails without explicit opt-in.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of any data we hold about you or your player
  • Request deletion of any session, report, or uploaded footage
  • Withdraw consent and have all associated data removed
  • Lodge a complaint with a relevant data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email support@pitchscout.ai with your session ID or report link and we will respond within 5 business days.

Security

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest. Access to production systems is restricted to authorised personnel only. We conduct regular reviews of our security posture and respond promptly to any identified vulnerabilities.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be noted at the top of this page with a revised date. Continued use of PitchScout AI after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at support@pitchscout.ai.
